# 🦅 Tariff Tycoon: The 3:00 AM Post
*A satirical macroeconomic incremental game of presidential executive orders, market shorting, and financial nihilism.*

---

## 📌 Elevator Pitch
> *"The S&P 500 opens in 4 minutes. Sign an 800% tariff on Canadian timber with your squeaking Golden Sharpie, short the forestry sector on Robinhood under your desk, and deploy DOGE auditors to balance the vibes."*

## 🕹️ Core Game Loops
1. **The Golden Sharpie Slam (The Clicker)**: Stamp executive orders with earth-shattering screen shake and ink splatter particles to levy arbitrary tariffs on countries and absurd goods.
2. **The 3:00 AM Post & Market Shorting (The Arbitrage Engine)**: Use your phone under the desk to buy leveraged put options, drop an unhinged 3:00 AM ALL-CAPS decree on social media to crash target stocks, cash out thousands of percent in gains, and tweet a walk-back to ride the call options rally.
3. **DOGE Liquidation Guillotine**: Balance inflation and sovereign debt by deploying efficiency auditors to liquidate federal agencies for quick liquidity.
4. **Ontological Phase Shift**: Late-game economic protectionism transcends Earth into taxing physical laws: 150% border duty on solar photons from the Sun, taxing imports from the future, and seizing the 2nd Law of Thermodynamics.
