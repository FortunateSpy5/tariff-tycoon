# 🦅 EXECUTIVE DEGEN: SHORT THE WORLD
*(The Art of the 3:00 AM Tariff)*

> *"The S&P 500 opens in 4 minutes. Sign an 800% tariff on Canadian maple slurry with your squeaking Golden Sharpie, short European automakers on BagHolder Pro under the Resolute Desk, and deploy D.U.M.P. auditors to balance the vibes."*

---

## 📌 Game Overview
**EXECUTIVE DEGEN: SHORT THE WORLD** is an unhinged, satirical macroeconomic incremental clicker that blends the tactile satisfaction of *Cookie Clicker* with the cosmic phase shifts of *Universal Paperclips* and the pure financial depravity of *r/wallstreetbets*.

You play as **"The Dealmaker-in-Chief"**—a silhouette with a glowing aerodynamic comb-over, a red power-tie that drags along the carpet, and hands that keep changing size.

---

## 🕹️ Core Gameplay Loops
1. **The Golden Squeak & Tantrum Slam (Primary Clicker)**:
   * Slam your Golden Sharpie onto executive orders with tactile screen recoil, procedural marker squeaks, and ink splatter particles.
   * Build the **Tantrum Meter** to detonate **CAPS LOCK FRENZY** (10x tap multiplier, infinite ink, flashing neon-red screen).
2. **The 3:00 AM Lethal YAP & Causal Insider Shorting**:
   * Pull out **BagHolder Pro** (the bootleg terminal under the desk).
   * Buy 1,000x leveraged Put options on a target sector.
   * Slam **[LAUNCH LETHAL YAP]** on **`YAP`** with procedurally generated 3:00 AM rants (*"FRUIT ECOSYSTEM INC. IS COOLING CHIPS WITH COMMIE TAP WATER! 400% PATRIOT TAX AT SUNRISE! SAD!"*).
   * Watch the stock plunge -40%, cash out +15,000% gains, and post a walk-back to ride the Call options rally.
3. **The D.U.M.P. Liquidation Tree (Austerity Guillotine)**:
   * Liquidate 10 federal agencies (NOAA, FDA, FAA, EPA, USPS, SEC, IRS, Federal Reserve) for massive instant cash—while dealing with hilarious catastrophic hazards (hurricanes, flammable tap water, 12% miss-mailed orders).
4. **The 4-Phase Escalation (Universal Paperclips Scale)**:
   * **Phase 1: The Customs Desk** ($0 to $1M) — Shaking down tourists at Terminal 4 JFK for unpasteurized French brie.
   * **Phase 2: The Oval Syndicate** ($1M to $100B) — Front-running global markets with late-night social media decrees.
   * **Phase 3: Fortress America** ($100B to $100Q) — Freezing maritime trade; naval container auctions; FDA reclassifying drywall as "High-Calcium Freedom Flour."
   * **Phase 4: Ontological Tariffs** ($10^{18} to $10^{42}$) — Expanding the Commerce Clause into cosmic physics: 45% tariff on the Sun's photons, 900% tariff on the Future, declaring the 2nd Law of Thermodynamics unconstitutional, until the universe files for Chapter 7 bankruptcy.
5. **The Viral 9:16 "Senate Hearing" Clip Generator**:
   * One-click export of split-screen vertical video for TikTok/X: top half shows a red-faced senator holding your tweet on a foam poster board, bottom half shows Subway Surfers and soap cutting.
6. **Streamer Mode (Twitch / Kick Integration)**:
   * Live chat spams `!YAP` to trigger tantrums, votes on international tariff targets, and buys corporate pardon plaques.
