# Legal Compliance & Parody Safety Guidelines

These rules apply whenever writing dialogue, naming entities, creating assets, or designing scenarios in **EXECUTIVE DEGEN: SHORT THE WORLD**.

---

## 1. Legal Doctrine: Transformative Parody & Fair Use
Under First Amendment jurisprudence (*Hustler Magazine, Inc. v. Falwell*, 485 U.S. 46 (1988) and *Campbell v. Acuff-Rose Music, Inc.*, 510 U.S. 569 (1994)), parody and satirical caricature are strongly protected forms of speech, provided they cannot reasonably be understood as stating actual factual claims.

To preserve absolute legal immunity:
1. **Never use living politicians' real names:** Use archetype titles and caricatures (*"The Dealmaker-in-Chief"*, *"Senator Hornswoggle"*, *"Prime Minister Jonas of the Great Northern Annex"*).
2. **Never use real corporate trademarks or logos:**
   - Apple $\to$ **Fruit Ecosystem Inc.**
   - Tesla $\to$ **GigaFlex Motors**
   - Boeing $\to$ **DoorPlug Dynamics**
   - Microsoft $\to$ **MicroSoftness Cloud**
   - Chipotle $\to$ **GuacSurcharge Grill**
   - Nvidia $\to$ **MicroSilicon Foundry**
   - S&P 500 $\to$ **The S&Pain 500 (`$PAIN`)**
   - Robinhood $\to$ **BagHolder Pro**
   - Truth Social $\to$ **`YAP`**
   - C-SPAN $\to$ **C-SNOOZE**
   - Subway Surfers $\to$ **Metro Sprinter 99: Endless Slop**
3. **Never use official government seals or verbatim agency acronyms:**
   - Do not use the real Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE).
   - Use **`D.U.M.P.`** (*Department of Unilateral Market Pruning*) or **`C.H.O.P.`** (*Chainsaw Harvesting of Outlay Programs*).
4. **Thinly veiled or composite sovereign nations:**
   - Canada $\to$ *The Great Northern Annex* / *Snowcumbia*
   - Mexico $\to$ *The Nearshore Federation*
   - France $\to$ *The Strike Republic*
   - Germany $\to$ *Overthinker Union* / *Das Rustbelt*
   - China $\to$ *The Red Factory* / *Temu Prime*
   - Taiwan $\to$ *Silicon Archipelago*
   - Denmark / Greenland $\to$ *Future State #52*
   - Switzerland $\to$ *The Secrecy Haven*

5. **Real place names must be unmistakable inventions.**
   A short, plausible-sounding proper noun reads as a real location even when the
   surrounding gag is clearly fictional. `Gate 99B` was a composite of JFK
   Terminal 4 and JFK Tower's "Gate 40s" numbering — a real gate number in a real
   airport, which is the one string in Phase 1 that a screenshot alone could not
   be distinguished from reportage.

   **Ruling:** the location is now **GATE 99B, THE DEEPLY TERMINAL ANNEX** — a
   name that is internally absurd and cannot be read as a real terminal.
   "Liberty International Airport" is likewise fictional and stays, but the gate
   no longer travels on its own.

   The general test: *could this string appear, unironically, on a sign in the
   real world?* If yes, it needs a joke welded onto it that could not survive a
   straight reading.

   This applies to **acronyms** as much as to names, and short ones are the
   worst offenders. The ten liquidatable agencies in `constants/agencies.ts` are
   invented — `N.O.C.L.O.U.D.` is not NOAA, `F.A.T.` is not FDA, `S.M.O.G.` is
   not EPA, `C.O.U.G.H.` is not CDC, and most are ordinary English words.

   The exception was `D.O.E.-N.U.K.E.`: `D.O.E.` is the real, exact, two-letter
   acronym of the US **Department of Energy**, and the expansion sat one word from
   the real one ("En**ergetic**" vs "En**ergy**"). Short + exact + near-legible is
   the combination to avoid. Renamed to `D.E.E.P.-N.U.K.E.`, keeping the pun.

   The test for an acronym: *is this exact letter sequence already in use by a
   real body?* If yes, change a letter — a near-miss is safe, a verbatim is not.

## 2. Platform Censorship Evasion Guidelines
Automated content moderation systems on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Meta sometimes misidentify satire as real-world election misinformation or financial fraud.

When generating automated viral export clips (the Senate Hearing generator):
* Automatically substitute sensitive terms with stylized satirical text (e.g. `T@riffs`, `Gl0bal Shaked0wn`, `Degen Short`).
* Use 3D stylized caricature avatars rather than photorealistic face-scans.
* Always water-mark exports with: `PARODY SIMULATION // NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE`.
