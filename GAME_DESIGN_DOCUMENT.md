# 🦅 Tariff Tycoon: The 3:00 AM Post
## Comprehensive Game Design Document (GDD)

---

## 1. High Concept & Thematic Pillars
* **Elevator Pitch:** An unhinged macroeconomic incremental clicker where players sign arbitrary tariffs with an ink-splattering Golden Sharpie, insider-trade market crashes on Robinhood under the Oval Office desk, and deploy DOGE auditors to liquidate federal agencies for scrap.
* **Thematic Tone:** Reality-TV kleptocracy, WallStreetBets nihilism, 3:00 AM social media chaos, and speculative financial absurdity.
* **Target Audience:** Fans of *Universal Paperclips*, *Cookie Clicker*, *AdVenture Capitalist*, financial meme culture (r/wallstreetbets), and political satire.

## 2. Core Currencies & Mathematical Architecture

### A. Currencies
1. **Treasury Vault ($):**
   * Generated from customs duties, tariffs, and insider stock market options profits.
   * Spent on Golden Sharpie upgrades, media surrogates, and buying foreign sovereign debt.
2. **Grievance Points (GP):**
   * Generated via active Golden Sharpie stamping and TV press conferences.
   * Spent on executive authority, social media reach, and deregulation decrees.
3. **National Debt ($):**
   * Counters upwards continuously. High debt causes inflation shocks and raises bond yields.
4. **Market Volatility Index (VIX):**
   * Spikes whenever a tariff decree or 3:00 AM post is published.
   * Multiplies stock option payouts and determines chance of wild market swings.
5. **Approval Rating (%):**
   * Fluctuates wildly. Below 20% triggers "Deep State Subpoenas"; can be restored by hosting arena rallies or mailing physical stimulus checks featuring your signature.

## 3. The 3-Act Unfolding Progression (Universal Paperclips Phase Shift)

### Act 1: The Oval Desk (Bilateral Consumer Protectionism)
* **UI Layout:** Mahogany desk, golden pen holder, ringing red phone, scrolling cable news ticker.
* **Targets:** Canadian timber, Mexican avocados, French cheese, Italian shoes, German luxury sedans.
* **Upgrades:**
  * *Tungsten Nib:* +10% Sharpie stamping power.
  * *Autopen Intern:* Automatically signs 1 minor tariff every 2 seconds.
  * *Fox Pundit Surrogate:* Auto-generates +15 Grievance Points/sec.

### Act 2: Trade War Supremacy & DOGE Liquidation
* **UI Layout:** Pentagon / Bloomberg Terminal hybrid. Global trade map dynamically tinting into tariff red.
* **The DOGE Chopping Block:**
  * Liquidate the Department of Education $\to +\$450\text{M}$ immediate liquidity.
  * Replace the National Weather Service with Sharpie maps $\to +\$85\text{M/sec}$ savings.
  * Sell national parks to private meme-coin mining consortiums.
* **Prestige 1: "Constitutional Convention / Midterm Wipe"**
  * Resets tariffs and debt in exchange for **"Executive Immunity"** tokens, granting permanent regulatory immunity and autopen bots.

### Act 3: Ontological Tariffs (Taxing the Fabric of Physics)
* **UI Layout:** Surreal cosmic imperial throne room. Space-time fabric covered in customs inspection stamps.
* **Cosmic Decrees:**
  * *Solar Photon Border Duty:* 150% tariff on sunlight entering national airspace.
  * *Temporal Import Tax:* 500% tariff on knowledge arriving from the future.
  * *Entropy Seizure:* 2nd Law of Thermodynamics declared unconstitutional and seized by the Treasury.
